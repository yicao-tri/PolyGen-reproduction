# Article goal — decision brief for Hisa

**Working title:** When does conditional polymer generation improve a physically validated material choice?

**Primary audience:** researchers using generative models for materials inverse design. Offline optimization and autonomous-discovery evaluation are secondary audiences. Polymer/electrolyte researchers become a core audience only if independent transport measurements support a physical explanation.

**Central question:** under one explicitly fixed electrolyte protocol, does continuous conditioning provide useful selection information beyond a nondegenerate binary condition and unconditional generation with the same screening budget—and can an independent physical evaluation distinguish that gain from evaluator or selection artifacts?

**Intended contribution:** a support-defined, budget-matched generator-to-physical-evaluation study that changes a concrete research decision: keep improving the generator, revise the evaluator, purchase independent physical labels, or stop. Success does not require continuous conditioning to win. It requires experiments that distinguish these explanations and preserve invalid, duplicate, infeasible and unmeasured outcomes.

## Scope and claim boundary

| Dimension | Role in this article |
|---|---|
| Property extrapolation | Primary: a conductivity improvement beyond a declared reference under a common fixed protocol. Independent measurement and boundary comparability are essential. |
| Chemical extrapolation | Novelty/support diagnostic. Exact structure novelty is not unseen-family generalization; the latter needs separate grouped data/checkpoints. |
| System extrapolation | DP/salt concentration/temperature remain fixed in the minimum article. A later specification-shift study is optional. |
| Evaluation transfer | Essential: does proxy-based nomination survive parameterization, construction and independent transport evaluation? |

The four minimum results are (1) what the evaluator can identify, (2) useful control gain after fair baselines, (3) distinct executable candidates supported by independent physical evaluation, and (4) a failure diagnosis that changes the next action. Finding a known RF range restriction alone supports an audit/module, not a full discovery paper. Beating a concurrently measured boundary polymer is not automatically beating every training polymer under a different protocol.

**First discriminating question:** can available evaluators use the same 100 verification slots to recover more *known high-label structures* than a nearest-neighbor baseline? An RF can fail the numerical crossing test yet retain useful ranking. We will examine ranking, value extrapolation and uncertainty separately. The first study is retrospective assay qualification—not a generator benchmark or a new prospective validation set.

**Conditional extension:** after the four results, choose **one** route: specification-induced rank reversal **or** independent feedback improving decisions. Neither is required automatically. No robust mechanism or independent gain means no scope expansion.

**What is not claimed:** a new generative architecture; proof that generative AI can/cannot extrapolate; agent planning ability; experimentally superior electrolytes; universal transfer across families/DP/formulations; or an already validated autonomous platform. Prior conditional generation/MD and offline evaluation work motivate the narrower contribution, rather than establish its novelty by themselves.

**Decision requested:** accept or revise the bounded first-round design and its provisional decision threshold; name an analyst and reviewer, and decide whether its limited compute should be funded. No model fitting, generation or MD is authorized or launched by this brief.
