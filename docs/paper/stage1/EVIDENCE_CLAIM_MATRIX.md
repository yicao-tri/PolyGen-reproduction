## Primary-method and prior-art boundary

- [Official tree regression criterion](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/tree.html#regression-criteria) and [RF prediction](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.RandomForestRegressor.html): ground the averaging/range argument; they do not imply zero ranking information.
- [Original PolyGen](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-024-01470-9) and [Design-Bench](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/trabucco22a.html): conditional material generation and offline evaluation are prior art. The intended contribution needs independent decision evidence beyond those components.
- New agent, electrolyte discovery, DP mechanism and self-improvement claims are outside the supported evidence. The earlier v2 package retains the broader literature/vision review; this session chooses a bounded first test rather than another literature expansion.
